# STROBE Checklist — Cross-Sectional Studies

**Manuscript:** *Objectively graded retinal disease, self-reported diagnosis, and eye-care utilisation in US adults (NHANES 2005–2008).*

**Checklist version:** This table uses the **official STROBE item numbering (items 1–22 with sub-items a–e)** from the STROBE Statement for observational studies (von Elm E, et al. *Strength in Numbers: Improving the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology — The STROBE Statement*. PLoS Med 2007;4(10):e296; the design-specific STROBE *cross-sectional* checklist). Item headings and sub-item wording follow the official checklist.

**Location column:** references are to the **actual submitted source files** by line number so they are locatable now, not deferred to an accepted PDF: `M##` = line in `manuscript/题目3手稿.md` (main text), `S##` = line in `manuscript/Supplementary_Materials_v7.md`. Where a section is cited by name the line range of that heading is given. If the files are re-flowed at typesetting these anchors must be re-generated from the final version. Where an item's **cohort- or case-control-specific text does not apply to a cross-sectional study**, it is marked *Not applicable* with the reason.
